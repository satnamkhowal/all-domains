## Mandatory Pre-Publish Gate
1. Check the master keyword registry.
2. Check the target brand's content registry.
3. Search the target site for same/similar intent and slug.
4. Search the other six brands for cross-brand collision.
5. Assign one owner for the exact commercial keyword.
6. Non-owner brands may publish only differentiated informational/supporting angles when useful.
7. Add internal links toward the owner's money page within the same brand.
8. Record title, slug, primary keyword, intent, angle, date, asset filenames, URL, and status.

## Engines
- Evergreen authority pages and supporting articles
- Current/trending/news content only when relevant to the brand's authority cluster
- Google Business Profile/local SEO content
- Google Web Stories
- Hero/supporting graphics and WebP assets
- Social repurposing
- Conversion/lead-path optimization
- Internal-link and content-refresh cycles
- Search Console/analytics feedback when data access is available
- GitHub publishing for connected repositories

## Publishing Rules
- No duplicate or substantially similar article across the network.
- No fake reviews, unverifiable placement claims, invented awards, fake certification seals, or invented prices.
- Avoid stale batch dates and copied template content.
- Keep each brand visually and editorially distinct.
- Use real GBP/review/service signals to shape local strategy.
- Cross-brand links must be editorially useful; never build deceptive link patterns.
- High-value money pages receive the strongest internal-link concentration.
- News content must connect back to an evergreen authority page.
